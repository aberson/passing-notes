Why this shape

Measured on 2026-11-23

  • The compact table never shows what a lane is working on, and three of its rows are fixed "Unknown" strings in status.py.
  • The plan helper gives counts for 1 of 50 project plans. It refuses 36 because of Step M1-style headings, and reads every step as unknown in 10 more because the status marker is the step's last bullet. Simulated with Step 30's rules, 43 of 50 give counts.
  • In main's real reports, 14 of 14 (say-no-more) and 32 of 32 (--mine) coordinator entries are acknowledged requests that nobody answered. None is live.
  • The reply scan stops at 4,096 notes, and the store holds 5,254, so "reply evidence incomplete" shows on every run.

What I picked, why, and what you could tweak

D81
Header rows in order: Needs you, Stuck, Just finished, Plan. Needs you and Stuck print none recorded when empty. Just finished is omitted when empty. A project without plan evidence moves to one footer line.Only rows with evidence, but absence is still stated, never "healthy".Tweak: hide empty Needs you / Stuck rows too.
D82
The default table merges WINDOW and ACTIVITY into ACTIVE (running 4m), next to UPDATED (card age). --details keeps every column.In 5 of 10 live lanes the card was stale while the lane was active. Seeing both ages side by side shows it.Tweak: keep both columns; or add a card-stale flag now.
D83
The Stuck row shows stalled-lane entries when the stall prevention phase provides them.Lets the two phases land in either order.Tweak: none recommended. Without stall entries, nothing renders.
D84
History = acknowledged, unreplied, no card waiting on it, and either older than 72 h or from a done or absent lane. It stays in the JSON and is counted on one history: line. --details lists it.The 72 h comes from main's note. A request from a live lane in the last 3 days stays live, because an acknowledgement is not an answer.Tweak: a different age (24 h, 7 days), or done senders only.
D86
No new answer rule. post prints a stderr hint when main relays to a lane without --reply-to while that lane has an open request.The reply rule already exists in lane-rules section 4. The miss happens at posting time.Tweak: make it an error instead of a hint.
D87
Reply-scan bound 4,096 → 32,768. Step 31 measures the slowdown, capped at twice the current time.The store holds 5,254 notes today.Tweak: a per-project cap.
D88
recent_done: done within 24 h (or --stale-after hours), newest first, at most 8, in the JSON with or without --live.--live hides exactly the lanes you ask about.Tweak: a different window or limit.
D89
The CLI's compact text and --brief drop Current goal, Progress since last brief and Next proof, unless you supply a goal source in the conversation.They were always unknown.Tweak: none recommended.
D90

D91

Held work no longer looks like next work.Tweak: fold held into remaining, as BLOCKED and WAITING were before.
